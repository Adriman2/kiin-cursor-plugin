# Attio walkthrough video — spoken script (~3–5 min)

Screen recording only. Show Attio + Kiin connect; no brand ad. Do not claim official LinkedIn partnership.

**On-screen title card (optional):** LinkedIn Ads Sync — powered by Kiin · Attio connect

---

**[0:00–0:30] Open on Kiin connected**

Say: “This is LinkedIn Ads Sync, powered by Kiin. We already have a LinkedIn Ads account connected. Next we connect Attio so we can match ads engagement to real pipeline.”

Show: MCP client or https://mcp.kiin.co/start with LinkedIn Ads connected; pick the account.

**[0:30–1:30] Start Attio OAuth**

Say: “From Kiin we open connect Attio. If the Attio workspace belongs to a client, we’d send them a shareable connect link instead. Same OAuth, no Kiin login on their side.”

Show: `connect_attio` authorize URL or connect page → Attio consent (read scopes visible).

**[1:30–2:15] Success + connection list**

Say: “After approval, Kiin stores the connection and starts the first sync. Read-only on Attio — we don’t write back to records.”

Show: success page → `list_crm_connections` with Attio active.

**[2:15–3:00] Sync + report**

Say: “Nightly sync keeps the mirror fresh. Admins can sync Attio now. Then we ask for influenced pipeline: companies and deals that saw LinkedIn exposure before the deal.”

Show: optional `sync_attio_now` → ask for influenced pipeline / deals matched to LinkedIn companies.

**[3:00–3:45] Attio glance**

Say: “Here’s the same company in Attio that showed up in the report. That’s the loop: LinkedIn Ads, Attio pipeline, influence in one place.”

Show: Attio company or deal record briefly.

**[3:45–4:00] Close**

Say: “Install LinkedIn Ads Sync powered by Kiin, connect Attio once, and ask your AI client for the report. Privacy and terms are on kiin.co.”

End on privacy/terms or product site.
